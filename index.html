Video Presentation Guardrails & Vocabulary Matrix

Eliminate apologetic, overly technical, or pushy language. Replace with authoritative consultative framing.

Weak / Avoid Saying Strong Consultative Replacement Strategic Impact
"This is a report..." / "Sorry to bother you..." "I reviewed your online presence and found three key revenue gaps..." Establishes instant authority without sounding like a cold vendor pitch.
"You don't know what you don't know" "This is a very common gap for established local leaders..." Lowers defensiveness and keeps the owner from feeling patronized.
"You guys are doing great" "You have a strong baseline foundation in [specific area]" Grounds praise in objective evidence rather than empty flattery.
"AI is important because it's new and trendy" "AI search is another key channel where high-value buyers discover providers" Focuses on commercial discovery rather than tech hype.
"There's a lot of money on the table" "This is an opportunity to capture high-intent commercial calls" Sounds professional and outcome-focused.
"You should probably do X..." "My strategic recommendation is to prioritize..." Positions you as a trusted advisor leading the strategy.
"You can do this yourself if you want..." (Apologetic) "You can implement this internally, or my team can execute it for you." Respectfully presents choice while maintaining service value.
"Ball's in your court" / "Let me know" "Use the sign-up link in the report to select your service, or let's map out a call." Provides a friction-free, unambiguous next action.
Option 1: Direct Store Link Close

"Now, you can take this report as a guide. But if you'd like us to handle this for you, simply click the sign-up link inside the report, select your tier, and we'll start immediately."

Option 2: Consultative Call Close

"If your goal is turning these gaps into consistent revenue, let me map this out for you. Let's set up a brief 15-minute call to review priorities, timeline, and ROI."

Option 3: Ultra Short (One-Liner)

"If you want us to fix these gaps for you, just click the sign-up link in the report, pick your preferred service, and we'll take it from there."
